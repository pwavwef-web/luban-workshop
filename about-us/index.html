Our Story

A restaurant built around craft, learning and welcome.

Luban Workshop Restaurant is more than a place to order a meal. Set at the University of Cape Coast, it is a dining space where authentic Chinese cuisine meets the energy of a real, working hospitality workshop.

Born from the Ghana–China Luban Workshop partnership, our kitchen is also a classroom. Every service gives our team the chance to practise the details that matter: careful preparation, steady timing, thoughtful presentation and attentive guest care — a restaurant shaped by both discipline and heart.
